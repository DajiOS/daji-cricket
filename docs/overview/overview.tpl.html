A website you can open and click through today, owner and staff screens, a super-admin console, Contacts, the academy and match scoring, full accounting with bank-statement import and automatic reconciliation, Ask AI, and a page that offers the software to other ground owners. 1. Summary

Daji Cricket Ground is a booking platform for cricket grounds. Customers find a ground, pick a date and time and pay an advance. Owners and staff run the grounds from a dashboard, keep their books, and keep in touch with everyone who has played. The super admin controls the global payment switch and platform settings. Your public website stays on Wix; the booking engine runs on its own server and appears inside your Wix pages.

8. The books and Reports

Every figure in Reports comes from one set of books that is worked out on demand from the real records: bank and cash transactions, booking payments, refunds, account credit, the bookings themselves, and any journal entries an owner types in. Nothing is stored twice, and every entry has equal debits and credits, so the reports cannot disagree with each other: the Balance Sheet always balances (assets equal liabilities plus equity), and the Future and Past schedules tie to the Profit and Loss and the Balance Sheet.

8.1 When a booking counts as income

A customer's advance is not income when it arrives: it is money held for a session not yet played. It becomes income on the play date.

The Books tab is laid out like a bookkeeper's workspace: a side menu, a + New menu for adding things, an overview of cards, a Bank transactions page with account cards and Pending / Posted tabs, a Books review page, and a Reports list with favourites. Every report has a Print button and an Export menu (PDF, Excel, CSV). Added reports: profit and loss by month and by quarter, customer balances with aging, audit log, general ledger.

8.2 The five reports

ReportWhat it shows
Profit and LossIncome and expenses for a month or any dates (up to two years). Accrual basis by default: a session is income on the day it is played. Cash basis is available too. Optionally compared with the previous period. Bookings income is shown without GST; advances kept on no-shows and late cancellations, and credit that expired, are shown separately
Balance SheetAs at any date: cash and bank, money online and by UPI not yet in the bank, booking balances customers owe you, equipment, then what you owe — customer advances for sessions not yet played, account credit owed to customers, refunds owed, GST payable, bills, loans — and owner equity. Unreviewed bank lines sit under "Needs attention" until categorised
Trial balance and ledgerEvery account with its debit and credit totals, and a line-by-line ledger for any one account for any dates, with where each line came from
Future scheduleConfirmed bookings still to be played (a session in progress is flagged), unpaid holds, how much of the money is already held as advances, credit outstanding, and refunds owed. Grouped by day, week (Monday to Sunday) or month, and by ground. Defaults to today and the next 90 days
Past scheduleSessions played and no-shows by play date, and cancellations by the day they were cancelled, with the money each earned: collected, still outstanding, advances kept, cancellations credited, refunded or kept, and credit that expired. The earned money is read from the same books, so it ties to the Profit and Loss. Defaults to the last 30 days
  • Exports: each report downloads as a PDF, an Excel workbook (with working totals) or CSV. The schedule CSV is the plain list of sessions. Text that looks like a spreadsheet formula is neutralised so a hostile narration cannot run in Excel.
  • Limits: a schedule covers at most 366 days at once; exports hold up to 20,000 rows.
  • Month sign-off: a month can be signed off once it is over and every bank line in it is categorised. A signed-off month is locked; reopening needs a reason. Mistakes in a signed-off month are fixed with journal entries, which are voided rather than deleted.
  • Manual journals: an owner can type in entries (depreciation, a loan, an owner's capital), but not into accounts that come from real data (bank, customer advances and credit, booking income), because a typed entry would break the tie to the real records.
  • Who sees it: read with view_reports or manage_accounting; change with manage_accounting.

11. How it is built, and how it works with Wix

Why the server sits outside Wix

Wix cannot run this project's Node server, and the booking guarantee (the database refuses two bookings for the same ground and slot, even if the application is wrong) and the accounting work (reading uploaded bank files, calling Yodlee) need a real database and server. So the system is split in two: Wix keeps the public pages (home, grounds, gallery, contact, academy), which Google can read, and the booking, account and accounting screens are added to Wix pages as custom elements loaded from api.dajicricket.com. The Expo app and the Razorpay webhooks talk to the same address.

PartChoice
BackendNode.js 22 with TypeScript, using only what ships with Node (node:http, node:sqlite, node:test). No outside packages, because the package registry cannot be reached from the build environment
DatabaseSQLite file with a daily backup (section 21), 39 tables. A Postgres migration set is planned for when the platform outgrows one server
WebsiteWix pages plus custom-element widgets (booking, my account, accounting with Contacts, Reports and Ask AI), built in the new look. The screens themselves already exist as plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript served by the server, and are what the widgets will wrap
MobileExpo (React Native). Source will be written here, but Expo cannot be installed in the build environment, so the app cannot be built or run here (section 14)
Shared codeOne package of types, money helpers and brand tokens used by the API, widgets and app, so all three agree on every field
HostingA small server running Docker and Caddy for automatic HTTPS, reachable at api.dajicricket.com (a DNS record added in Wix)
ErrorsEvery error has the same shape: a code, a message and, for bad input, the list of fields at fault

Security design

  • Passwords are hashed with scrypt; login tokens are signed and can be revoked by bumping a per-user version.
  • Every request checks role and permission on the server; owner data is separated by owner, and another owner's record answers "not found" rather than "forbidden".
  • Razorpay webhooks are signature-checked over the raw bytes and processed once; direct UPI cannot confirm a booking without an authorised verifier.
  • Updates to customers are consent-checked per channel, previewed, confirmed by number, capped per day and de-duplicated; unsubscribe links are signed.
  • Ask AI only reads, only for the owner in question, never sends personal details to Claude, and every amount in a Claude reply is checked.
  • Month-end locks stop quiet changes to figures that have been signed off.
  • Rate limits on sign-in and registration; request size limits; uniform validation errors.
  • Demo accounts and the fake payment gateway exist only on localhost or when explicitly turned on; the server warns loudly if demo mode is on for a real domain.
  • Optional two-step sign-in with an authenticator app (6-digit codes, 30-second steps) for owners, staff and the super admin.
  • Browser headers: HSTS for two years, no framing, no content sniffing, same-origin opener policy, strict referrer policy.
  • Uploaded receipts and statements are size-limited, type-checked, stored per owner and never served to another owner.
  • Secrets come from environment variables, never from code. The Anthropic key, Razorpay keys and the login secret are all settings.
Each of these is exercised by automated tests in this build, except where section 14 says otherwise. Section 19 lists what is still open.

21. Backups and everyday operation

  • Daily backup at 03:15 India time: the database and the uploaded files (receipts), written to a temporary folder, integrity-checked, then renamed so a half-written copy is never mistaken for a good one. The newest 14 are kept. The admin desk shows the schedule and the latest snapshot.
  • A backup on the same disk does not protect against losing the disk. Copy the backups folder off the server too, and encrypt it first.
  • Restore is a manual copy of the snapshot back into place. It has not been rehearsed on a second machine; do that once before launch.
  • Daily sweep inside the server: releases unpaid holds after checking for late payments, expires credit, sends reminders, posts recurring entries and takes the backup.
  • Manual sends and opt-out: staff can mark a contact as opted out of a channel; manual-send mode prepares messages to be sent by hand and records them as sent.
