Privacy policy

Applies to the website dajicricket.com and the Daji Cricket Ground phone app. Project by Daji Parivar. Last updated 17 October 2026.

Who we are

Daji Cricket Ground and Daji Sports, Surat, and its academy MP13 Academy run by Munaf Patel. The person responsible for your data is Hemanshu Patel, 7567156000, dajicricket@gmail.com.

What we collect and why

WhatWhy

We do not sell your data and we do not show advertising. We send WhatsApp, SMS or email reminders only if you agreed to them, and you can change that in your profile.

Who else sees it

Only the people who need it to run your booking: the ground's staff and owner. Service providers process data for us under their own terms: Amazon Web Services (hosting, India region where available), Razorpay (payments), Meta WhatsApp and MSG91 (sign-in codes and reminders), Google (email), Expo (push notifications) and Anthropic (Ask AI answers). Bank statements of the business, not of customers, may be read through a bank-feed provider.

How long we keep it

Your account details are kept while your account exists. Booking and payment records are kept for as long as the ground must keep its accounts (usually 8 years under Indian law), without your name once you delete your account.

Your choices, including deleting your account

In the app: Profile, then Delete my account. Deleting removes your name, email, mobile number, password, picture, notification token and sign-in. Past bookings stay in the ground's records as "Deleted user". Cancel upcoming bookings and switch off auto-debit first.

If you cannot sign in, ask us to delete it: see how to request deletion. You can also ask us to correct your details or give you a copy of them.

Children

The academy keeps details of young players, given by a parent or guardian. Parents can ask us to correct or delete them at any time.

Security

Connections are encrypted. Passwords are hashed. Sensitive fields are encrypted in the database. Access is limited by role. No system is perfectly secure; tell us at once if you think your account was misused.

Your rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

We process your personal data with your consent or for the lawful purposes the Act allows, such as giving you the booking you asked for and meeting legal duties. You may withdraw consent at any time (this will not affect what was done before), ask for a summary of the data we hold, ask us to correct or erase it, and nominate a person to exercise these rights if you die or cannot act. Write to the grievance officer below. We reply within 15 days. If you are not satisfied you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Data is stored on servers that may be outside India. We transfer it only to the providers listed above, for the purposes above. We do not track children or show them targeted advertising. Where a child's data is given for the academy, we rely on the consent of the parent or guardian.

If a breach of personal data happens, we will tell the Data Protection Board and the people affected as the law requires.
