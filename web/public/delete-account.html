Delete your account

In the app

  1. Open Profile.
  2. Scroll to Delete my account and press it.
  3. Enter your password (if you have one), type DELETE, and confirm.

Cancel upcoming bookings and switch off auto-debit first.

If you cannot sign in

Email dajicricket@gmail.com from the address on your account, or call 7567156000 from the mobile number on your account, and say you want it deleted. We will delete it within 7 days and confirm.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: your name, email, mobile number, password, profile picture, notification token and sign-in.
  • Kept without your name: booking and payment records, because the ground must keep its financial records for the period the law requires (usually 8 years). They show "Deleted user".
